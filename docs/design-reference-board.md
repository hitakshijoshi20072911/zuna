# Zuna design reference board

This board records inspiration, not copied layouts or assets.

## Lenis — https://lenis.dev/
- Study: scroll pacing, progressive reveals and oversized type that rewards continued scrolling.
- Zuna use: chapter-aware background changes, restrained parallax and reveal-on-entry on the homepage.
- Original treatment: Zuna combines the motion with marketplace actions, offer previews and native scrolling.
- Do not copy: source code, exact composition, brand marks, copy or assets.

## Good Earth — https://www.goodearth.in/
- Study: bold colour discipline, tactile visual entrances and editorial image scale.
- Zuna use: the short tiled Zuna preloader, deep bean panels and saturated chapter transitions.
- Original treatment: a nine-tile price-tag motif resolves into Zuna's marketplace tagline.
- Do not copy: loader sequence, imagery, typography, layout or marks.

## The House of Things — https://thehouseofthings.com/
- Study: curated product presentation, whitespace and product storytelling.
- Zuna use: the editorial product gallery and clear business/category metadata.
- Original treatment: the catalogue is broader than decor and adds offer affordances.
- Do not copy: product photography, copy, layout or brand identity.

## Nicobar — https://www.nicobar.com/
- Study: contemporary Indian lifestyle restraint and calm hierarchy.
- Zuna use: warm surfaces, concise labels and quieter transactional screens.
- Original treatment: Zuna adds a visible jade interaction accent and negotiation narrative.
- Do not copy: styling, imagery, marks or exact layout.

## Etsy India — https://www.etsy.com/in-en/
- Study: breadth of categories and discovery vocabulary.
- Zuna use: category-led marketplace navigation across tech, fashion, home, gifts and handmade goods.
- Original treatment: categories are shown in an editorial story and demo data is labelled.
- Do not copy: marketplace UI, text, product imagery or platform marks.

## Verification note

References were used as stated design direction for this static implementation. No third-party asset is hotlinked by the application.
